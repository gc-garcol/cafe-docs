1. Prometheus

  
Prometheus pulls metrics over HTTP from targets it discovers through the Kubernetes API, and stores them as time series. Queries use PromQL. Useful sources are node-exporter, cAdvisor, kube-state-metrics (object state) and your app's /metrics. Do not confuse them with metrics-server, which only feeds kubectl top and the HPA.

2. Grafana

  
Dashboards and exploration over many data sources, including Prometheus, Loki and Tempo. Build dashboards around the golden signals: latency, traffic, errors and saturation. Keep them in Git as code.







  
3. Logging

  
Containers write to stdout and stderr. The runtime stores them on the node, and kubectl logs reads them. The files vanish with the Pod, so run a node agent as a DaemonSet, such as Fluent Bit or Promtail, to ship logs to a central store like Loki or Elasticsearch. Prefer structured JSON logs.







  
4. OpenTelemetry

  
A vendor-neutral standard for metrics, logs and traces. SDKs and auto-instrumentation produce data. The Collector receives it over OTLP, processes it and exports it to any backend. The OpenTelemetry Operator can inject instrumentation into Pods.







  
5. Distributed Tracing

  
One request crosses many services. A trace is the whole journey, made of spans, one per operation, linked by parent ids. A header such as W3C traceparent carries the trace id between services. Traces show where the time goes. Backends include Tempo and Jaeger.







  
6. Alertmanager

  
Prometheus rules decide when to fire. Alertmanager decides what to do with them. It groups related alerts into one notification, inhibits alerts that a bigger failure explains, silences known ones, and routes by label to Slack, email or a pager. Alert on symptoms users feel, not every cause.







  
7. SLI / SLO

  
An SLI is a measured indicator, such as the share of requests that succeed under 300 ms. An SLO is the target for it, such as 99.9% over 30 days. The error budget is what is left to fail. Alert on how fast the budget burns, and let the remaining budget guide how fast you ship.
